### Exercise 5. Matrix Times Vector
Let

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 \\\\
1 & 3 \\\\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad x=
\begin{pmatrix}
4 \cr
2
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Compute $Ax$. Then express $Ax$ as a linear combination of the columns of $A$, with coefficients coming from the vector $x$.

### Solution 5

$$
Ax = \begin{pmatrix} (2)(4) + (-1)(2) \cr (1)(4) + (3)(2) \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 8 - 2 \cr 4 + 6 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 \cr 10 \end{pmatrix}
$$

Expressed as a linear combination of the columns of $A$:

$$
Ax = 4 \begin{pmatrix} 2 \cr 1 \end{pmatrix} + 2 \begin{pmatrix} -1 \cr 3 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 8 \cr 4 \end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix} -2 \cr 6 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} 6 \cr 10 \end{pmatrix}
$$
