### Exercise 1. Determinant of a 2×2 Matrix
Compute
det(3 -2 \\ 5  4), det(1  7 \\ 2 14).
Which matrix is invertible? Justify your answer.

Solution:
det(A) = (3 * 4) - (-2 * 5) = 12 + 10 = 22
det(B) = (1 * 14) - (7 * 2) = 14 - 14 = 0
The first matrix is invertible because its determinant is non-zero (22 ≠ 0).
