### Exercise 1. Determinant of a 2×2 Matrix
Compute

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & -2 \cr
5 & 4 \cr
\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 7 \cr
2 & 14 \cr
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Which matrix is invertible? Justify your answer.

### Solution 1
For the first matrix:

$$
\det \begin{pmatrix} 3 & -2 \cr 5 & 4 \end{pmatrix} = (3)(4) - (-2)(5) = 12 - (-10) = 22
$$

For the second matrix:

$$
\det \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 7 \cr 2 & 14 \end{pmatrix} = (1)(14) - (7)(2) = 14 - 14 = 0
$$

The first matrix is invertible because its determinant is non-zero ($22 \neq 0$). The second matrix is not invertible because its determinant is equal to zero.
