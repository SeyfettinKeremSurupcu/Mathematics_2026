Exercise 2. Determinant of a 3×3 Matrix

Using Sarrus' rule:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\det A &= (1)(3)(5) + (2)(4)(2) + (-1)(0)(1) - ((-1)(3)(2) + (1)(4)(1) + (2)(0)(5)) \\
       &= 15 + 16 + 0 - (-6 + 4 + 0) \\
       &= 31 - (-2) \\
       &= 33
\end{aligned}
$$

Check using cofactor expansion along the first column:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\det A &= 1(15 - 4) - 0 + 2(8 - (-3)) \\
       &= 11 + 2(11) \\
       &= 33
\end{aligned}
$$
