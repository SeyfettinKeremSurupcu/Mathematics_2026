### Exercise 1. From a System to a Matrix

The system can be written as the matrix equation $Ax=b$ where:

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 1 \\
1 & 3 & -2 \\
3 & 1 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
x=
\begin{pmatrix}
x \\
y \\
z \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$$
b=
\begin{pmatrix}
4 \\
1 \\
7 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

The coefficient matrix $A$ contains the multipliers of the variables. The vector of unknowns $x$ contains the variables in order. The right-hand side vector $b$ contains the constants from the right side of the equals signs. Matrix multiplication $Ax$ takes the dot product of each row of $A$ with the vector $x$, perfectly recreating the original left-hand side of each equation.
