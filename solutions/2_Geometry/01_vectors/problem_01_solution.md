### Exercise 1. Vector Operations and Their Meaning

For $u=(2,-1,3)$ and $v=(-1,4,2)$ compute the following operations:

$$
u+v, \qquad u-v, \qquad 3u-2v
$$

Then verify that $(u+v)-v=u$, and explain geometrically what adding two vectors means.

**Answer 1**

Vector operations:

$$
\begin{aligned}
u+v &= (2-1, -1+4, 3+2) = (1, 3, 5) \\
u-v &= (2 - (-1), -1-4, 3-2) = (3, -5, 1) \\
3u-2v &= (6, -3, 9) - (-2, 8, 4) = (8, -11, 5)
\end{aligned}
$$

Verification:

$$
(u+v)-v = (1, 3, 5) - (-1, 4, 2) = (2, -1, 3) = u
$$

**Geometric Interpretation:** Adding two vectors means placing the tail of one vector at the head of the other to find the net displacement (the tip-to-tail rule).
