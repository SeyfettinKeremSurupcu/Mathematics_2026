### Exercise 5. Angle Between Vectors

Given $u=(1,1,0)$ and $v=(1,0,1)$:

1. Use the sign of $u\cdot v$ to predict the type of angle.
2. Compute the angle.
3. Compare the result with the prediction.

**Answer 5**

Dot product:

$$
u\cdot v = (1)(1) + (1)(0) + (0)(1) = 1
$$

Since the result is positive, the angle is an acute angle.

Angle calculation:

$$
\cos\theta = \frac{u\cdot v}{\|u\|\,\|v\|} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}\sqrt{2}} = \frac{1}{2}
$$

The angle that satisfies this equation is $\theta = 60^\circ$ (or $\frac{\pi}{3}$). Since $60^\circ$ is an acute angle, it perfectly matches the initial dot product prediction.
