### Exercise 1. Recognizing Curves from Canonical Form

Recognize the curves

$$x^2+y^2=9,$$

$$\frac{x^2}{16}+\frac{y^2}{9}=1,$$

$$y^2=8x,$$

$$\frac{x^2}{9}-\frac{y^2}{4}=1.$$

For each one, identify the most important features and make a sketch. Explain which parts of the equation let you recognize the type of curve.

#### Solution 1

1. **Circle:** $x^2+y^2=9$
   - **Recognition:** Both $x^2$ and $y^2$ have equal positive coefficients ($1$) and a positive constant ($r^2=9$) on the right-hand side.
   - **Features:** Center $(0,0)$, radius $r=3$, intercepts $(\pm 3,0)$ and $(0,\pm 3)$.

```text
          y
          ^
        3 +   .---.
          |  /     \
    -3 ---+-(   O   )---+---> x
          |  \     /    3
       -3 +   '---'
```

2. **Ellipse:** $\frac{x^2}{16}+\frac{y^2}{9}=1$
   - **Recognition:** Sum of squared terms with unequal positive denominators ($16 \neq 9$) equal to $1$.
   - **Features:** Center $(0,0)$, semiaxes $a=4$ ($x$-axis) and $b=3$ ($y$-axis), intercepts $(\pm 4,0)$ and $(0,\pm 3)$.

```text
          y
          ^
        3 +    .-------.
          |  /           \
    -4 ---+-(      O      )---+---> x
          |  \           /    4
       -3 +    '-------'
```

3. **Parabola:** $y^2=8x$
   - **Recognition:** One variable is squared ($y^2$) and the other is linear ($x$).
   - **Features:** Vertex $(0,0)$, axis of symmetry $y=0$, opens to the right ($x \ge 0$), passes through $(2,4)$ and $(2,-4)$.

```text
          y
          ^       . (2,4)
          |     /
          |   /
    ------+--+----------> x
          |   \
          |     \
          |       ' (2,-4)
```

4. **Hyperbola:** $\frac{x^2}{9}-\frac{y^2}{4}=1$
   - **Recognition:** Difference of squared terms equal to $1$ (opposite signs on $x^2$ and $y^2$).
   - **Features:** Center $(0,0)$, vertices $(\pm 3,0)$, semiaxes $a=3$ and $b=2$, asymptotes $y = \pm \frac{2}{3}x$.

```text
          y
          ^
      \   |   /
       \  |  /
     )  \ | /  (
    -3---\|/---3---> x
     )  / | \  (
       /  |  \
      /   |   \
```
