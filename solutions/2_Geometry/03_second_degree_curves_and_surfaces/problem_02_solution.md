### Exercise 2. Circle by Completing the Square

Reduce

$$x^2+y^2-6x+4y-3=0$$

to canonical form. Find the center and radius, then substitute two simple points from the circle into both forms of the equation.

#### Solution 2

Complete the square for $x$ and $y$:

$$\begin{aligned} (x^2-6x) + (y^2+4y) &= 3 \\ (x-3)^2 - 9 + (y+2)^2 - 4 &= 3 \\ (x-3)^2 + (y+2)^2 &= 16 \end{aligned}$$

- **Canonical form:** $(x-3)^2 + (y+2)^2 = 4^2$.
- **Center:** $(3,-2)$.
- **Radius:** $r=4$.

Verification with two points on the circle:

- Point $(7,-2)$:
  - Canonical form: $(7-3)^2 + (-2+2)^2 = 16 + 0 = 16$.
  - General form: $7^2 + (-2)^2 - 6(7) + 4(-2) - 3 = 49 + 4 - 42 - 8 - 3 = 0$.
- Point $(3,2)$:
  - Canonical form: $(3-3)^2 + (2+2)^2 = 0 + 16 = 16$.
  - General form: $3^2 + 2^2 - 6(3) + 4(2) - 3 = 9 + 4 - 18 + 8 - 3 = 0$.
