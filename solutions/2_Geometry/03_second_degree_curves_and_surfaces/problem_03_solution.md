### Exercise 3. Ellipse

For

$$\frac{x^2}{25}+\frac{y^2}{9}=1,$$

find the semiaxis lengths, the intercepts with the coordinate axes, and make a sketch. Explain along which axis the ellipse is more stretched and how this is visible in the equation.

#### Solution 3

- **Semiaxis lengths:** $a = \sqrt{25} = 5$ along the $x$-axis, $b = \sqrt{9} = 3$ along the $y$-axis.
- **$x$-intercepts ($y=0$):** $(-5,0)$ and $(5,0)$.
- **$y$-intercepts ($x=0$):** $(0,-3)$ and $(0,3)$.
- **Orientation:** The ellipse is more stretched along the $x$-axis because the denominator under $x^2$ ($25$) is greater than the denominator under $y^2$ ($9$), so $a = 5 > 3 = b$.
