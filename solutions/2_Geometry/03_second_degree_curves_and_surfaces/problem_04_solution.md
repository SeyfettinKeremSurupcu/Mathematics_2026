### Exercise 4. Parabola

For

$$y^2=12x,$$

identify the vertex, axis of symmetry, and opening direction. Find points on the parabola for $x=3$ and use them to make a sketch.

#### Solution 4

- **Vertex:** $(0,0)$.
- **Axis of symmetry:** The $x$-axis ($y=0$).
- **Opening direction:** To the right (positive $x$-direction), since $y^2 \ge 0$ implies $x \ge 0$.
- **Points for $x=3$:**

$$y^2 = 12(3) = 36 \implies y = \pm 6$$

The points are $(3,6)$ and $(3,-6)$.

```text
          y
          ^         . (3,6)
          |       /
          |     /
          |   /
    ------+--+------+---> x
          |   \     3
          |     \
          |       \
          |         ' (3,-6)
```
