### Exercise 7. Classifying a Second-Degree Equation

Determine the type of object described by:

1. $4x^2+9y^2=36$,
2. $x^2-y^2=1$,
3. $x^2-4y=0$,
4. $x^2+y^2+1=0$.

In the last case, explain why there are no real points.

#### Solution 7

1. **Ellipse:** Dividing by $36$ yields $\frac{x^2}{9}+\frac{y^2}{4}=1$.
2. **Hyperbola:** $x^2-y^2=1$ is a rectangular hyperbola with vertices $(\pm 1,0)$ and asymptotes $y=\pm x$.
3. **Parabola:** $y=\frac{1}{4}x^2$ is a parabola with vertex $(0,0)$ opening upward.
4. **Empty set (no real points):** Rewriting gives $x^2+y^2=-1$. For all $x,y \in \mathbb{R}$, $x^2 \ge 0$ and $y^2 \ge 0$, so $x^2+y^2+1 \ge 1 > 0$. No real pair $(x,y)$ satisfies the equation.
