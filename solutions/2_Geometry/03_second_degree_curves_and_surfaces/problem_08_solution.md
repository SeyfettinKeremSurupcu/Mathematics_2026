### Exercise 8. Sphere

Reduce

$$x^2+y^2+z^2-4x+2y-6z-2=0$$

to canonical form. Find the center and radius. Give two points on the sphere and check them in the equation.

#### Solution 8

Complete the square for $x$, $y$, and $z$:

$$\begin{aligned} (x^2-4x) + (y^2+2y) + (z^2-6z) &= 2 \\ (x-2)^2 - 4 + (y+1)^2 - 1 + (z-3)^2 - 9 &= 2 \\ (x-2)^2 + (y+1)^2 + (z-3)^2 &= 16 \end{aligned}$$

- **Canonical form:** $(x-2)^2 + (y+1)^2 + (z-3)^2 = 4^2$.
- **Center:** $(2,-1,3)$.
- **Radius:** $r=4$.

Verification with two points on the sphere:

- Point $(6,-1,3)$: $6^2 + (-1)^2 + 3^2 - 4(6) + 2(-1) - 6(3) - 2 = 36 + 1 + 9 - 24 - 2 - 18 - 2 = 0$.
- Point $(2,3,3)$: $2^2 + 3^2 + 3^2 - 4(2) + 2(3) - 6(3) - 2 = 4 + 9 + 9 - 8 + 6 - 18 - 2 = 0$.
