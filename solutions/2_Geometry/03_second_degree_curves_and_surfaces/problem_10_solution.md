### Exercise 10. Paraboloids

Compare

$$z=x^2+y^2$$

and

$$z=x^2-y^2.$$

For each surface, describe the cross-sections $x=0$, $y=0$, and $z=c$, then make a qualitative sketch.

#### Solution 10

**Surface 1: Elliptic (Circular) Paraboloid** $z=x^2+y^2$

- $x=0$: $z=y^2$ (parabola opening upward).
- $y=0$: $z=x^2$ (parabola opening upward).
- $z=c$: $x^2+y^2=c$ (empty for $c<0$, point $(0,0,0)$ for $c=0$, circle of radius $\sqrt{c}$ for $c>0$).

```text
          z
          ^
       \  |  /
      (===+===)   z = c > 0 (circles)
        \ | /
         \|/
    ------+------> x, y
```

**Surface 2: Hyperbolic Paraboloid (Saddle)** $z=x^2-y^2$

- $x=0$: $z=-y^2$ (parabola opening downward).
- $y=0$: $z=x^2$ (parabola opening upward).
- $z=c$: $x^2-y^2=c$ (hyperbola along $x$ for $c>0$, intersecting lines $y=\pm x$ for $c=0$, hyperbola along $y$ for $c<0$).

```text
          z
          ^
       \  |  /    z = x^2 (upward)
        \_|_/
        / | \     z = -y^2 (downward)
       /  |  \
```
