### Exercise 13. Intersection of a Sphere and a Plane

Given the sphere

$$x^2+y^2+z^2=25$$

and the plane $z=3$, find the equation of the intersection curve and its radius. Then describe what happens for $z=c$ depending on $c$.

#### Solution 13

For $z=3$:

$$x^2+y^2+9=25 \implies x^2+y^2=16$$

The intersection is a circle in the plane $z=3$ centered at $(0,0,3)$ with radius $r=4$.

For general $z=c$, the intersection satisfies

$$x^2+y^2 = 25-c^2$$

- $\vert{}c\vert{}<5$: circle of radius $r=\sqrt{25-c^2}$ centered at $(0,0,c)$.
- $\vert{}c\vert{}=5$: single tangent point $(0,0,\pm 5)$.
- $\vert{}c\vert{}>5$: empty set (plane does not intersect the sphere).
